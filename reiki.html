Spiritually guided life-force energy, for health and happiness.

In Japanese, Rei means universal and spiritual consciousness, and Ki means life force. Reiki is a traditional method for activating and balancing that cosmic energy within you. It's transferred through an attunement from a Reiki Master — once received, it stays with you for life.

The foundation

Usui Reiki

Reiki guides itself with its own wisdom — it isn't taught the way other healing techniques are. It's transferred to the student by a Reiki Master during an attunement, a process that opens the crown, heart and palm chakras and creates a lasting link between the student and the Reiki source.

Once you've received a Reiki attunement, you have it for the rest of your life. Reiki can help at every level — physical, mental and spiritual — and attunement can even be given at a distance. It's simple enough that anyone, even a child, can learn and practise it, working through symbols given by the Master that begin working the moment they're activated.

The Yin to Usui's Yang

Violet Flame Reiki

Violet Flame Reiki is a gentle, feminine, and powerful form of healing energy — the Yin aspect to the Yang of Usui Reiki. Channelled together, the two create a stronger, more all-encompassing healing experience.

Practitioners often describe a cool sensation and a "softer" energy during a Violet Flame session. That softness doesn't mean it's less effective — Violet Flame Reiki is very strong and acts quickly. Its vibration is simply felt differently than Usui Reiki's.

A Tibetan lineage

Lama Fera

Lama Fera is a powerful energy-healing system practised by Tibetan Lamas for generations. "Lama" means teacher — the equivalent of the Sanskrit word "guru" — and "Fera" refers to the circle of energy drawn around the body.

The life-force energy in Lama Fera is said to radiate from Gautama Buddha, channelled through the healer and directed toward illness using symbols traced with a gem wand. It's used to support spiritual growth, deepen meditation, ease pain from chronic conditions, clear stagnant energy from spaces, calm fear and anxiety, and improve memory and visualization. Sacred mantras are repeated throughout to open a space where healing can take place.

The Reiki of Compassion

Karuna Reiki

Karuna means "an action taken to diminish the suffering of others." Karuna Reiki is healing energy that helps awaken universal compassion and wisdom within — and when combined with that state of consciousness, compassion becomes a powerful transformational force.

Karuna Reiki draws on a collection of additional symbols beyond those used in other systems, with its own distinct attunement and energy. Chanting and toning are used to deepen the healing process — the vibration of the voice acts as a carrier wave, helping the energy penetrate more deeply into the body.

Clearing what blocks abundance

Money Reiki

Money Reiki is specifically about your relationship with money — helping you earn and manage it more effectively, in ways that support your highest good. It won't promise your money problems solved overnight through positive thinking alone.

Rather than manifestation, Money Reiki is about clearing negative energy around money, making it a natural complement to whatever prosperity or abundance work you're already doing. As that negative energy clears, the attracting elements of Money Reiki can take hold — though results vary from person to person, depending on what's been carried forward.

Aligning the seven centres

Chakra Balancing

Chakra balancing is energy healing focused on directing energy into the seven chakras — the energy body, not just the physical one. It's a deeply meditative experience, bringing the body's seven main chakras into alignment through a fluid combination of massage, specific essential oil blends, and healing crystals.

The result is a calm, harmonious state that leaves clients feeling grounded and with an increased overall sense of wellbeing.

Guided by angelic energy

Angel Reiki

Angel Reiki is a holistic healing practice that combines traditional Reiki with a focus on angelic guidance. Healing energy is channelled from practitioner to recipient with the intention of supporting physical, emotional, and spiritual wellbeing.

What sets it apart is the inclusion of angels and angelic energies in the process — practitioners often invoke angels, archangels, and other celestial beings during a session, believed to offer protection, support, and healing. It's holistic by nature, addressing physical, emotional, mental and spiritual aspects together, and often encourages a deeper connection to your own spirituality along the way.
