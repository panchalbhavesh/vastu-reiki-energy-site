01

Usui Reiki

＋

Usui Reiki is a Japanese spiritual practice centred on the idea of life-force energy. In a session, practitioners use their hands lightly on or above the body. Ask Pulkit how he conducts sessions and discuss your preferences about touch before booking.

Receiving a Reiki session is different from learning Reiki or receiving an attunement. This page describes the practices offered for sessions. Contact us separately if you want to ask about learning.

02

Violet Flame Reiki

＋

Violet Flame Reiki uses violet-flame imagery within an energy-based spiritual practice. Some practitioners combine it with Usui Reiki.

Ask how this approach would be used in your session. Sensations and personal experiences vary; a particular result is not promised.

03

Lama Fera

＋

Lama Fera is presented within Tibetan-inspired spiritual traditions. The practice uses symbols, mantras and a wand as part of its ritual.

Ask Pulkit what this involves and whether it fits your spiritual preferences. It is not a treatment for chronic pain, anxiety or other medical conditions.

04

Karuna Reiki

＋

Karuna Reiki places compassion at the centre of its spiritual practice. Some approaches include symbols, chanting and vocal toning.

If you are interested, ask which elements are included and tell your practitioner about your comfort with sound or touch.

05

Money Reiki

＋

Money Reiki is a spiritual practice focused on beliefs and feelings about money. It can be explored as personal reflection, without promises of increased income or financial results.

It does not replace budgeting, debt support or advice from a qualified financial professional.

06

Chakra Balancing

＋

Chakra balancing draws on the traditional belief in seven energy centres. Sessions may include meditation and symbolic use of crystals or oils.

Ask which elements are used before booking, especially if you have sensitivities or preferences about oils, scents or touch.

07

Angel Reiki

＋

Angel Reiki combines Reiki with beliefs about angelic guidance. It is an option for people who connect with that spiritual tradition.

Ask how the practice is conducted and decide whether it suits your beliefs and preferences. Experiences are personal and results vary.
