The foundation

Usui Reiki

The original Japanese system. A Reiki Master passes the ability to a student in an attunement that opens the crown, heart and palm chakras — and once received, it stays for life. It works in person or at a distance, and it's simple enough that a child can learn it.

The Yin to Usui's Yang

Violet Flame Reiki

A cooler, softer current that practitioners pair with Usui for a fuller session. Clients often notice a cool sensation rather than warmth. Don't mistake that for weakness — it moves quickly and reaches deep.

Tibetan lineage

Lama Fera

Practised by Tibetan Lamas for generations. ‘Lama’ means teacher; ‘Fera’ is the circle of energy traced around the body. Symbols drawn with a gem wand and repeated mantras direct the energy — used for chronic pain, anxiety, clearing a space, or deeper meditation.

The Reiki of compassion

Karuna Reiki

Karuna means acting to ease someone else's suffering. This system uses its own symbols and attunement, and adds chanting and toning — the voice acts like a carrier wave so the energy settles in more deeply.

Clearing what blocks abundance

Money Reiki

Not manifestation, and not positive thinking. Money Reiki clears the negative energy that builds up around money so your own efforts to earn and manage it stop working against you. How long that takes depends on what you're carrying.

Aligning the seven centres

Chakra Balancing

A slow session focused on the energy body: each of the seven chakras in turn, with massage, essential oils and crystals. Most clients leave grounded rather than wound up — the goal is a steadier baseline, not a single big shift.

Guided by angelic energy

Angel Reiki

Traditional Reiki with angelic guidance layered on top. The practitioner still channels the energy; the difference is who else is invited in. Many clients say it deepens their own sense of spiritual connection.
